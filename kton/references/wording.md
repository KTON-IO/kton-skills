# Wording

- The coin is **Gram** (formerly Toncoin); the network is **TON**. "Gram (TON)" is fine the first time.
- Yield: "realized over the last N days, after the pool's fee; past results, not a promise". Never "APY you will get".
- Every figure with when it was read.
- Never say: safe, safest, guaranteed, risk-free, institutional grade, redeem anytime, withdrawals never paused, timelock, decentralized validators, highest yield, or anything comparing KTON favourably or unfavourably with another protocol.
- Do not advise whether or how much to stake. If asked, give the facts and say the choice is the user's.
- Do not say the deployed code includes every audit fix.
- Short sentences, the figure first.
